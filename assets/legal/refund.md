The App may, now or in the future, offer optional paid items — such as coin packs or other one-time products — and **subscriptions** that renew automatically. When such purchases are offered:

## 4. How Refunds Are Handled

Because purchases are processed by Google Play, refunds are primarily governed by **Google Play's refund policies**:

- Google Play generally lets you request an automatic refund for a short window after purchase (currently around 48 hours) directly through the Play Store.
- To request a refund, use the Google Play refund process — open the Play Store, go to your **Order history**, select the item, and choose **Refund** — or visit the Google Play Help Center at https://support.google.com/googleplay/answer/2479637.
- Refund eligibility, timelines, and outcomes are ultimately determined by Google under its policies.

If you were charged in error, an item was not delivered, or you have another concern that Google Play cannot resolve, contact us at **prantadutta1997@gmail.com** and we will review it in good faith.

## 5. Subscriptions — Renewal and Cancellation

For any subscription offered through the App:

- Subscriptions **renew automatically** at the end of each billing period until you cancel them.
- You can **cancel at any time** in Google Play → Subscriptions. Cancelling stops future renewals; you keep access until the end of the period you have already paid for.
- Except where required by law or by Google Play policy, amounts already paid for the current period are generally **non-refundable**, but your access continues until that period ends.
- If we change a subscription price, Google Play will notify you and, where required, ask you to confirm before the new price takes effect.
